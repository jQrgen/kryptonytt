  16. Government considers a temporary ban on new crypto data centres (written question 15:3707)

    Stortinget · 18 Sep 2026 BitcoinRegulation

    Sofie Marhaug (Red Party) asked when the government will act on crypto mining.   17. Verdiendringer i bitcoin tynger NBX

  18. Norway Wants to Ban Bitcoin Mining. The Miners Already Left | Rachel Geyer, EBEA

    Bitcoinpolitisk institutt Norge (podkast) · 17 Sep 2026 BitcoinRegulation

  20. USA letter på reglene for aksjehandel

    Finansavisen · 17 Sep 2026 · may require a subscription RegulationBlockchain

    The US Securities and Exchange Commission has adopted a temporary five-year arrangement exempting trading platforms for tokenised shares from being classified as exchanges, Finansavisen reports, citing Reuters. The tokens must carry the same rights as ordinary shares, the issuing company must be notified and can object, and synthetic products are not allowed.
