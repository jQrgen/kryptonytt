Changelog

Changes to Kryptonytt Norge: new pages, sections and features. Newest first. Daily news is not listed here.

  1. 3 Oct 2026

    The org chart now has more people in public professional roles from the Brønnøysund Register, new organisations (including from Kaupr’s Onchain Pages, checked against their own websites and public registers), a new “International players in Norway” category in the industry map, and public profile links for some people. Kaupr is a sponsor; see the About page.

  2. 3 Oct 2026

    New page: “How the rules are made”, an animated flowchart of how crypto rules for Norway are made and enforced, with a source for every step.

  3. 3 Oct 2026

    New industry map under “Who’s who”: organisations grouped by category, with logos and links to their org chart entries.

  4. 3 Oct 2026

    New page: Academia, with courses, student initiatives, publications and research groups on blockchain and crypto at Norwegian universities and university colleges.

  5. 3 Oct 2026

    The site is now in Nynorsk as its main language, with Bokmål and English as options at the top of every page. Your language choice is stored in a cookie.

  6. 3 Oct 2026

    The calendar now has a “Past events” section: finished events move there automatically and are never deleted.

  7. 3 Oct 2026

    New page: a changelog of changes to the site.

  8. 3 Oct 2026

    A notice that the news is updated daily by artificial intelligence, above the news list and at the bottom of every page.

  9. 3 Oct 2026

    Launch: front page with news, calendar, org chart (“Who’s who”), sources, about page and a screen mode for office displays.

The source code and full history are on GitHub.
